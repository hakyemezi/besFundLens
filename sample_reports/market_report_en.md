## Interpretation Notes

- `Flow` represents estimated investor net flow as a percentage of starting AUM.
- `Market Effect` represents the estimated AUM impact of fund return.
- `AUM Change` is approximately the sum of Flow and Market Effect.
- `Dominant Regime` shows the largest AUM-weighted market-flow quadrant within each archetype.
- `Strength` indicates whether the dominant regime is clearly concentrated or fragmented.
- Look-through-heavy funds should be analyzed with additional underlying fund exposure data.
